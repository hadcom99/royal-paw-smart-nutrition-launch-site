How it works

From animal profile to measurable health outcomes

1

Select your animal's profile

Enter your animal's species, local breed, age, and wilaya through the Royal Paw app or website. The platform instantly matches your input against validated Algerian physiological and climate data — not generic European defaults.

2

Receive a precision formulation

Royal Paw generates a formulation recommendation co-reviewed by Algerian veterinarians and priced between DZD 2,200 and DZD 3,500 per 2kg. Every recommendation cites the specific local dataset records it draws from, so you can verify the science.

3

Scan and contribute data

Each bag ships with a QR code that logs a behavioral nutrition data point back into Algeria's first proprietary animal feed dataset. Your purchase automatically improves the accuracy of future formulations for every Royal Paw customer.

4

Track measurable health outcomes

The Royal Paw app monitors coat, energy, weight, and digestion markers over time, correlating outcomes against formulation data. Vets and owners receive monthly reports showing precisely which nutritional adjustments are producing measurable results.
